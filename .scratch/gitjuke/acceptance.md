## Confirmed handoff threshold

The handoff threshold is met: the human approved the final acceptance ticket, all decision tickets are resolved, no in-scope fog or contradictory current specification statement remains, each confirmed contract has a traceable acceptance scenario/method, and prototype approval/assets plus evidence limitations are documented. ## Confirmed window and access acceptance

Minimum reader content viewport: 960×640 logical pixels (excluding native title-bar chrome), resizable above that. Stacks remains the default. At compact width or 200% text zoom, provide explicit list/detail/back navigation rather than squeezing all panes. Navigation, tabs, dialogs, reading-mode controls and essential actions remain reachable by keyboard/VoiceOver without clipping or focus traps. View-local vertical scrolling is allowed; horizontal scrolling belongs to code/long content, not the overall app navigation. Dialogs fit or scroll within the viewport. System reduced motion and the confirmed continuous/paged reading modes remain supported. This is an approved acceptance requirement, not evidence the fixture or Tauri shell already meets it.

## Confirmed evidence and release rule

Every in-scope matrix scenario below is required before a distributable v1 release, with its detailed subcases inherited from the linked decision. Boundary/permission/unavailable states pass by exhibiting the agreed honest limitation; they do not replace successful evidence for supported normal flows. Core coverage cannot be skipped because a fixture, account permission, reference machine or signing access is unavailable; record Blocked and defer release, or ask for an explicit scope/spec revision. Existing agreed exclusions are not missing tests or grounds for adding features. Optional syntax highlighting need not be implemented; when enabled it must meet its confirmed budgets.

Record each subcase as Pending, Pass, Fail or Blocked with build/schema version, OS/hardware/gh/API version where relevant, fixture identity/permission profile, procedure and an evidence link. No secrets/private payloads belong in public evidence; redact appropriately and keep authorized evidence local. A grouped row is Pass only when all required subcases pass for the declared matrix. Human prototype approval is a separate recorded fact, not a native test result. Logs/counters may use a controllable fixture environment without turning production into a log-persistence feature.

After implementation is separately authorized, validate high-risk API/gh/native-stack/auth/process/login feasibility early, before extensive UI integration. For release, replay the required deterministic fault/boundary suite and validate real authorized API/native flows plus the exact signed artifact. Re-run affected gates when build, schema, permissions, dependencies, OS support or signing identity changes; retain unaffected evidence only with a documented applicability assessment. Fixture-only tests cannot establish live provider or packaged OS compatibility.

Any Fail/Blocked required scenario prevents release under the current contract. Fix it within the spec, or obtain human approval for a named specification revision and update its decision/matrix/claims before continuing. Do not silently reduce watch coverage, weaken identity/purge/durability/privacy rules, relax performance limits, change 50-message paging or substitute a faster benchmark machine. A supported denied/expired source state is not a defect when the agreed fallback is correct. OS notification delivery/indexing/network latency remain qualified as agreed, rather than becoming new universal guarantees.

## Confirmed fixture and evidence scope

Minimum live coverage requires public and authorized private repository reads for the one configured GitHub.com identity, authored and direct/team-requested PRs, a mention on another PR, a published native stack and a confirmed nonmember, fork/deleted-or-unavailable-head cases, general comments/textless reviews/resolved-outdated threads, and Actions/external CI outcomes with retries. Do not fabricate a pass because an account lacks team-request or stack fixtures; record Blocked until evidence or an explicit scope revision exists. Fixture provisioning/account changes are separate future work and not authorized by confirmation of this planning policy.

Performance and lifecycle evidence records all approved conditions; OS/API/CLI/signed-artifact compatibility is checked against the exact released versions. Preserve generic preview policy even in demonstrations. A known provider/API incompatibility blocks that supported feature; a correctly displayed expected source-denied/missing/limited state can pass its negative-path scenario.
